Munkatiya's position is interpolated along the Sonprayag–Gaurikund road at ~1.5 km before Gaurikund (its reported distance in news coverage) — no surveyed GPS pin was available. Bhimbali and Linchauli positions are interpolated from published trek-distance markers (~7 km and ~9.5 km from Gaurikund), not GPS-surveyed. Sonprayag, Gaurikund, and Kedarnath coordinates are from public geographic records. Occupancy figures are estimated, not from real checkpoint-scan data. Production version replaces all of this with GSI-surveyed segment boundaries and real Char Dham checkpoint GPS + scan data end-to-end.

Live event log

This replays the real landslide that struck Munkatiya on the Sonprayag–Gaurikund road at approximately 21:16 IST on 19 May 2026, blocking a ~900 m stretch and triggering an SDRF/NDRF evacuation of 10,450+ pilgrims over 19–20 May with zero fatalities. Rainfall values above are fetched live from Open-Meteo's historical weather archive (ERA5 reanalysis) for Munkatiya's coordinates on that date — not hand-entered. Occupancy and risk-transition timing are an estimated reconstruction built around the confirmed event timeline, not a real per-checkpoint feed.

Correction from an earlier version of this prototype: a checkpoint called "Silai Band" was used previously but does not correspond to any real, verifiable location — it has been removed and replaced with Linchauli, a real trek waypoint. Munkatiya was also previously placed past Gaurikund on the foot trek; verified news coverage places it on the motorable road between Sonprayag and Gaurikund, and this version corrects that.
